# Chordal Hold's Misshifts

Chordal Hold's misshifts come from three sources:
- Chordal Hold deliberately allows some **same-side misshifts**, to avoid interfering with modifier chords, and is used with a relatively high tapping term to reduce these.
- The high tapping term increases **opposite-side misshifts**, and Permissive Hold is added to manage these.
- Permissive Hold's permissiveness creates **extra misshifts on both sides**.

Chordal Hold users are encouraged to learn a staccato typing style to mitigate these issues. Lightshift asks for no adjustment to typing style, but instead an adjustment to typing modifier chords.
